Creating an account is simple! Click the "Login" button in the navigation bar, then select "Sign Up". Fill in your details including name, email, and password. Once you verify your email address, you'll have full access to all our features including saved searches, property alerts, and your personal dashboard.

Typically, you'll need: valid government-issued ID, proof of income (pay stubs, tax returns), bank statements, pre-approval letter from your lender, and employment verification. Our agents can provide a complete checklist and guide you through the documentation process to ensure a smooth transaction.

The timeline varies, but typically takes 30-60 days from offer acceptance to closing. This includes home inspection (7-14 days), mortgage approval (30-45 days), and final closing procedures. Cash buyers can often close faster, sometimes in as little as 2-3 weeks. Our team works to expedite the process while ensuring all legal requirements are met.

Log into your account and navigate to "List Property" in your dashboard. Fill in the property details, upload high-quality photos, and provide accurate descriptions. Our team reviews each listing within 24 hours to ensure quality and accuracy. Premium listings with professional photography and virtual tours receive priority placement.

We offer flexible pricing plans: Basic listings are free with standard features. Premium plans ($99-$299/month) include featured placement, professional photography services, virtual tours, and enhanced analytics. We also offer commission-based options where you only pay upon successful sale (typically 2-3% of sale price).
